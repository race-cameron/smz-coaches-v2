Your own wood artwork goes in this folder.

Save your images with exactly these names and the vault will use them
in place of the wood it draws by itself:

  floor.jpg     wooden plank floor          (square)
  wall.jpg      carved wooden wall panel    (tall, about 9:16)
  ceiling.jpg   dark ceiling timbers        (square)
  door.jpg      vault door                  (tall, about 3:5)

Any image that is missing is simply skipped.
